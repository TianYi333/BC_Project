============================================================
一、新增：心跳报文 uptime 字段（设备运行时间）
============================================================
1. 字段语义
   - 键名 "uptime"，整型，单位秒（截断取整，误差 ≤1s）
   - 取值 HAL_GetTick()/1000（TIM7 1ms 时基），开机为 0 点，约 49.7 天回绕
2. 修改后心跳报文示例（device_status 为油泵协议 8 字段）：
   {"type":"heartbeat","device_id":"...","device_status":{"liquid_level_pct":...,
    "oil_temp_c":...,"pressure_mpa":...,"sensor_status":...,"sys_fault_bit":...,
    "sys_start":...,"sys_warning":...,"valve_state":...},"seq":42,
    "ts":1795133180,"uptime":86400,"sign":"A1B2C3D4"}
3. 代码改动（UserCode/Drivers/ETH/net_comm_task.c / .h）
   - HeartBeatParam_t 新增成员 uint32_t uptime_sec（结构体末尾追加，不影响既有字段偏移）
   - FillHeartBeatData()：memset 后新增 hb_param->uptime_sec = HAL_GetTick() / 1000U;
   - tcp_send_heartbeat() 签名临时对象：在 "type" 键之后追加 "uptime" 键
     （验签原文按键名字典序：device_id → device_status → seq → ts → type → uptime）
   - tcp_send_heartbeat() 最终报文：在 "ts" 之后、"sign" 之前插入 "uptime"
4. 协议契约影响（重要）
   - 心跳 HMAC-SHA256 验签原文新增 "uptime":N，网关侧签名计算必须同步纳入该键，
     否则升级后所有心跳 sign check failed。需与网关同版本发布。

Heartbeat gains integer-seconds "uptime" (= HAL_GetTick()/1000, zero at boot, wraps
after ~49.7 days). HeartBeatParam_t appends uptime_sec at struct end (no offset change
for existing fields); FillHeartBeatData samples it; tcp_send_heartbeat inserts
"uptime" after "type" in the sorted signature string and between "ts" and "sign" in
the message. The gateway MUST include "uptime" in heartbeat signature computation.
